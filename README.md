 About My Time Capsule Website – “Dear Future Me”

I created Dear Future Me, a simple, interactive website that works like a digital time capsule. It allows users to write letters to their future selves, record their dreams, choose their current mood, and preserve a little snapshot of who they are today. The idea behind it is to give people a way to look back at their thoughts, feelings, and hopes in the future.

While making the website, I learned how HTML, CSS, and JavaScript work together to create a functional webpage. One of the challenging parts was understanding how to make the different features interactive and save the user's writing. I wanted the website to feel personal, welcoming, and easy to use rather than overly complicated. I am proud that I could turn a simple idea into something interactive and meaningful while learning through the process.
